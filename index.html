Questions

The things agents ask.

Is it an exact copy of the house?

No, and we don't claim it is. The geometry is built from the floor plan so layout and room sizes are right; finishes are matched to the photography; anything the photos don't show is estimated. Every walkthrough carries that note.

How do I put it on Rightmove or Zoopla?

Paste the link into the listing's virtual tour field, exactly as you would a 360° tour. It also embeds on your own website and sends fine over WhatsApp.

What do you need from me?

The listing link, or the floor plan image and the photos. Multi floor homes: one plan per floor.

Who's behind Wander?

It's built in Dundee by a civil engineering student who checks every walkthrough personally before it goes out. Small on purpose, so you always deal with the same person.
